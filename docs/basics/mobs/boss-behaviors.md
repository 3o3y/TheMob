# Boss behaviors

Configured modes include existing aggressive/passive/flee behaviors and tactical bruiser, ranged, guardian, berserker and vanilla modes. The coordinated Zombie King specializes these mechanics per phase.

Actions use windup/cooldown windows, line-of-sight checks, arena leashes and bounded central processing. Cold suspension cancels pending attacks rather than replaying them on wake-up. Creative/spectator players and NPCs are not ordinary combat targets; marked Pets can remain valid combat entities.

Toggle TheMob abilities in `/mob` → **Skills**; individual switches include cleave, lunge, stomp, retreat, phase abilities/buffs/effects and minion summons.
