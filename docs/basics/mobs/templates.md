# Templates

The original YAML templates ship unchanged in the plugin. Copy a template through the admin menu or into your own file, then choose a unique `mob-id`. Keep the original sample as a reference.

- [Basic mob template](../../examples/mob_template.yml.md)
- [Boss template, phases and drops](../../examples/boss_template.yml.md)
- [Generated catalogue settings](../../examples/catalog.yml.md)
- [Rare/Epic equipment templates](../../examples/equipment.yml.md)
- [Zombie King behavior profile](../../examples/zombie-boss.yml.md)

On older Minecraft releases, replace unsupported vanilla entity/material identifiers in your own files. The generated catalog already filters unavailable content. [Definition guide](mobs.md).
